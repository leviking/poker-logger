Moving website history to iPhone

On the website, open Hand History and choose Export CSV. In the iPhone app, open Hand History and choose Import CSV. The original website history stays in your browser until you delete it there.

Finding and removing your entries

Tap the small hand count on the iPhone app's display to open Hand History. From there you can export your entries or delete the history and statistics. The website's Hand History page has its own export and reset controls.

AI review availability

The optional coaching exercise needs Apple Intelligence on a supported iPhone. The regular hand history and review summary remain available without it.
