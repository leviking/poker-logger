Poker Log

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Poker Log is a hand-tracking tool available as an iPhone app and a website. You can use either version without creating an account.

Hand history

The iPhone app saves your hands, outcomes, optional ending streets, and statistics in its local app storage. The website saves your entries in your browser's local storage. Neither version sends your hand history to a Poker Log server.

You can choose to copy, export, import, or share a CSV file. The destination you choose when sharing a file handles that copy under its own privacy terms. Your device's backup settings may also include locally stored app data.

Optional AI review

On supported iPhones, the post-game review can use Apple's on-device language model to suggest a study exercise from your saved hand summary. Poker Log does not send that summary to a developer-operated server.

Website requests

Visiting the website causes your browser to request site files from its host, which may process ordinary technical request information such as your IP address. The website uses a service worker to cache files for offline use. It does not use advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Support messages

If you contact support, we receive the information you send, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to your request.

Your choices

In the iPhone app, use Hand History to export or delete your entries. On the website, use Hand History to export or delete entries stored in that browser. You can also remove website data through your browser's site-data settings. For privacy questions, contact us through the support page.
